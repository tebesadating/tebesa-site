Frequently asked questions

What is Tebesa Dating?

A dating bot and mini app inside Telegram. You swipe, match and chat without installing anything.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Just open the bot in Telegram.

Will other users see my Telegram username?

No. Your Telegram ID and username are never shown.

Which languages are supported?

Amharic, English, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya and Somali.

How do I pay for Gold?

With Telebirr or Telegram Stars. Prices are 50, 200 and 600 ETB, or 150, 450 and 1,150 Stars.

Is it for adults only?

Yes. Tebesa is for people aged 18 and over.
